# Remo

A free, native Roku remote for macOS. Works with every Roku TV and streaming player on your network.

- Remote, keyboard typing and app launcher, in a window or your menu bar
- Tell Remo what to watch: on-device AI finds it and plays it
- Headphones mode: hear the TV on your Mac
- Automations, scenes and a sleep timer
- Siri, Shortcuts and Raycast
