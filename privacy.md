# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Remo does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

- **Your TVs:** Remo talks directly to the Roku devices on your local network. Nothing passes through our servers.
- **AI requests:** "Tell Remo" runs on your Mac with Apple's on-device model. Your words never leave your Mac.
- **Finding titles:** when you search for a show or video, Remo looks up the title name on public catalogs (JustWatch and YouTube) to find where it streams. Only the search text is sent; nothing that identifies you.
- **Settings** stay on your Mac.
