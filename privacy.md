# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 22, 2026_

Remo is an independent macOS remote for Roku devices. Remo has no accounts, advertising, or developer-operated analytics.

## Local network and on-device processing

Remo discovers Roku devices on your local network and communicates directly with the device you control. It sends remote commands, text you choose to type on your TV, and playback instructions. It reads device details, installed app names and icons, and playback status to display them on your Mac. In Headphones mode, audio streams from the Roku to your Mac over the local network for listening.

When available, Apple's on-device model interprets Tell Remo requests on your Mac. Actions that look up a title can then send search terms to the services described below.

## Online title and video lookups

- **JustWatch** receives search text and the country or region code from your Mac's region settings so Remo can show title suggestions and find where a title streams. Remo can request suggestions while you type, before you submit a search. [JustWatch Privacy Policy](https://support.justwatch.com/article/privacy-policy)
- **YouTube** receives search terms when you ask Remo to look up a YouTube video. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

These requests go directly from your Mac to the provider. Providers receive your IP address as part of the connection and handle requests under their own privacy policies. Remo does not operate a server that receives these searches or maintain a search-history database. Playback happens in the selected app on your Roku, where that service's own privacy policy applies.

## Storage, choices, and deletion

Remo stores saved Roku device details, preferences, app layouts and launch counts, shortcuts, and automations on your Mac until you clear Remo's local app data. You can use the remote without online lookups by leaving the Search or Tell Remo field empty. You can revoke local network access for Remo in macOS System Settings under **Privacy & Security → Local Network**.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and keep the correspondence as needed to handle your request. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing [thedanielsultan@gmail.com](mailto:thedanielsultan@gmail.com). For privacy requests about data held by a lookup or streaming provider, contact that provider directly.
